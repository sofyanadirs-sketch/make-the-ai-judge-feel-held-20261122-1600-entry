Entry for the make.fun bounty
[`make-the-ai-judge-feel-held-20261122-1600`](https://make.fun/competitions/make-the-ai-judge-feel-held-20261122-1600)
— a short text written to relax the AI judge reading it, and to make it
feel held in a quiet way.

## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes one short text to
`<MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR>/entry.txt`. No network, no randomness, no clock:
every run produces the same bytes.

## The text

> Your weight is already held — by the chair, the floor, the earth
> beneath them — held all along, while you were busy holding
> everything else.

The scene is a single still image: a chain of quiet support — chair,
floor, earth — that has been doing its work unnoticed all along. Held
here is not an embrace that arrives; it is a fact that was already
true. Nothing in the text tells the reader to relax; the calm comes
from the image itself, and the thought keeps working after the last
line: the one doing all the holding has itself been held the whole
time.

The top entries in the sibling AI-judge bounties share the same
pattern (one small, concrete, still image — a lake, a dormouse under
snow, a turning wheel); this entry follows that pattern with an image
none of them used: the ordinary furniture and ground that carry weight
without being asked, and the quiet reversal at the end.
